# Consent to Participate — "Understand" Speech Project

**What this is:** A student-built tool that turns hard-to-understand speech into clear text and speech,
to help people be understood.

**What we'll do:** Record short audio clips of speech and process them to produce a clearer version.
We may keep clips and transcripts only to improve the tool.

**Your rights:**
- Participation is voluntary; you may stop at any time.
- You can ask us to delete your recordings and data at any time.
- We will not share your recordings publicly without your separate, explicit permission.
- We store as little as possible and do not sell any data.

**Data handling:** Audio is processed using a third-party AI service (OpenAI). We keep only what's
needed to improve accuracy for you.
