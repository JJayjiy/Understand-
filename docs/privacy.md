# CoHear — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

CoHear does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server operated by the developer.

## What happens on your phone

**Audio.** The microphone is used only while you have tapped Listen. Audio is processed on the device by a speech-recognition model and is not recorded to disk or sent anywhere.

**Transcripts.** What you say is held in memory for the current session so you can read, edit, show, or speak it. It is not saved between launches. "Clear everything said" in Settings removes it immediately.

**Voice characteristics.** To tell your voice apart from other people in the room, CoHear computes a small numeric summary of your voice (pitch and spectral shape). It is held in memory only, is not a recording, cannot reconstruct your speech, and is discarded when the session is cleared or the app quits.

**Settings.** Your text size, cleanup, and voice-filter preferences are stored on the device.

## The one network request

On first launch, CoHear downloads its speech model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This request contains no audio, no transcript, and no personal information; like any download, the server sees your IP address. Hugging Face's privacy policy applies to that download: https://huggingface.co/privacy. After the download, CoHear does not need a network connection.

## Optional on-device cleanup

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, CoHear can use Apple's on-device language model to tidy a transcript. This runs on the phone. Apple's privacy terms for Apple Intelligence apply.

## Children

CoHear does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. It has no account and no data collection.
