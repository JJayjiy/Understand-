# CoHear — Support

CoHear is a speech-to-text app for people with dysarthria. It runs entirely on your iPhone. Your voice is never uploaded.

**Contact:** jingyulei.2016@gmail.com. I read every message and reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**The first launch is stuck on "Setting up CoHear."**
The app downloads its speech model once (about 500 MB). This needs Wi-Fi and a few minutes. After that it works fully offline. If it fails, check your connection and tap Try again.

**It doesn't hear me.**
Make sure CoHear has microphone access: iPhone Settings → CoHear → Microphone. While listening, the ring around the Listen button fills with your voice level; if it stays empty, the phone isn't picking you up — move closer or try without a case over the mic.

**It marked my own words "not you."**
The voice filter learns your voice from the first few things you say. Tap "This was me" on the line and it re-learns. You can also turn the filter off in Settings → Other voices.

**The words are wrong.**
Tap any line to fix it. Recognition of dysarthric speech is hard and the model makes mistakes; this is a research tool, not a clinically validated system.

**Does it save what I say?**
No. The transcript lives only while the app is open. "Clear everything said" in Settings wipes it immediately.
